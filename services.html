What We Do

Three Jobs We Do
Better Than Anyone Else

Peak Roofing has been climbing ladders in Denver neighborhoods for years. Whether your roof needs a full tear-off, storm repair, or new gutters, our crew shows up when they say they will, explains what they find on the roof, and leaves your yard cleaner than they found it.

Roof Replacement

When patching won't cut it anymore, we tear off the old roof down to the deck and build it back right with shingles rated for Colorado hail and wind.

  1. 01Full inspection and a written estimate, no surprises later
  2. 02Tear-off, deck repair, and disposal of the old roof
  3. 03New underlayment, ice and water shield, and shingles
  4. 04Full cleanup with a magnetic nail sweep of the yard

Storm Damage Repair

Denver hail doesn't ask permission. We inspect for hidden damage after a storm and help you get the repair covered instead of paid for out of pocket.

  1. 01Free storm inspection, often the same week
  2. 02Emergency tarping if the roof is actively leaking
  3. 03Photo documentation and help filing your claim
  4. 04Repair or replacement once the claim is approved

Gutter Installation

Old gutters pull away from the fascia and dump water right where you don't want it. We fit seamless aluminum gutters cut to your roofline, not off a shelf.

  1. 01Measure the roofline and check pitch and drainage
  2. 02Fabricate seamless gutter on site to fit exactly
  3. 03Install with proper slope and secure downspouts
  4. 04Optional guards to keep aspen leaves out come fall
